A Perfect Car-Free Weekend Day-Trip to Palo Alto

Looking for a relaxing weekend day-trip in the Bay Area without the stress of driving and parking? Palo Alto is one of the most pedestrian-friendly destinations on the Peninsula, making it the perfect spot for a car-free Saturday.

Step Off and Explore University Avenue

The moment you step off at the Palo Alto Caltrain Station, you are literally steps away from University Avenue—the vibrant heart of downtown. Start your morning by grabbing a world-class espresso at Verve Coffee Roasters or Coupa Café, both just a 5-minute walk from the platform.

Walking the Stanford Campus

After enjoying downtown, head in the opposite direction from the station towards the Stanford University campus. The beautiful Palm Drive leads you straight into the heart of the university. It’s flat, highly walkable, and lined with stunning architecture and outdoor sculptures.

  • Cantor Arts Center: Entrance is free! It features one of the largest collections of Rodin sculptures outside of Paris.
  • Stanford Shopping Center: If shopping is more your speed, it’s located right next to the station.

With Caltrain's reliable weekend schedule, you can spend your whole day exploring and hop on a train back home whenever you are ready. Say goodbye to traffic and hello to a perfect weekend!
